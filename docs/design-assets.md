# French Bakery visual assets

The UI combines original code-native SVG product illustrations with one decorative editorial image. The SVGs identify bread and pastry families across the dashboard, sales, purchasing, freshness, clustering and stock screens.

## Editorial image

- File: `public/assets/bakery-editorial.jpg`
- Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, then encoded as a JPEG for web delivery (approximately 354 KB).
- Purpose: decorative dashboard header. It is an illustration of the bakery theme, not a photograph of actual inventory.
- No external image service is required at runtime.

### Generation prompt

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: decorative editorial photograph for the right side of a minimal French bakery decision support dashboard. Create one wide landscape 3:2 photograph, no text. Warm quiet artisan boulangerie still life: a beautifully laminated golden croissant, two rustic baguettes with delicate flour, and a small pain au chocolat on a folded natural oatmeal linen napkin on a pale warm cream stone counter. Composition: subjects gathered toward right center with generous uncluttered pale ivory negative space on the left third; gentle side daylight, refined natural shadows, close overhead 35 degree view. Restrained palette: warm wheat gold, oatmeal, ivory, subtle sage cloth accent. Real appetizing flaky pastry texture, upscale food editorial photography. Clean scene, no people, no logos, no packaging, no text, no frames, no UI, no watermarks. All breads fully visible, avoid extreme blur.
